## What to draw

Adult woman. Long wet black hair fading to neon-pink. Pale rain-wet skin. Hot-pink void eyes. Cracked thorn / rose-crystal halo. Pink lightning heart-sigil on the sternum. Night rain. Hungry. Not a mascot.

Unknowable marks allowed: extra eyes on halo, hair, throat, shoulders. Long tongue. Dark-silk tentacles threaded with warm pink lightning. Do not explain them.

## Motion notes

- Loop the rain.
- Blink extra eyes out of time.
- Let the tongue arrive late.
- Heart-sigil first, then lightning.
- Halo never becomes a clean saint-ring.
